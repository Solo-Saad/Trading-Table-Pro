Privacy Policy for TradingTablePro

Last Updated: May 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how data is handled when you use TradingTablePro (the "Software").

We believe in complete privacy and user transparency. TradingTablePro is a standalone desktop application that runs entirely on your device. We do not collect, monitor, store, or transmit your personal data or trading records to any external servers.

1. Information Collection and Storage

TradingTablePro operates purely as a local workspace tracking tool.

  • Local Storage Only: Any information you enter into the application, including currency pairs, patterns, wave analysis, market direction, entry signals, and trade outcomes, is stored exclusively on your local device.
  • Database File Path: Trading records are stored in an embedded SQLite database located within your local application data directory, typically under %LOCALAPPDATA%/TradingTablePro/ on Windows systems.
  • No Remote Telemetry: We do not operate cloud services, remote databases, analytics systems, or tracking technologies. Your data never leaves your computer.

2. Information Usage

Information stored locally is used solely to provide application functionality:

  • Load previously saved trading records.
  • Save trading records entered by the user.
  • Generate local statistical summaries and trade analysis.
  • Maintain application functionality and data persistence.

3. Data Protection and Retention

  • Your Responsibility: Because all data remains on your device, protecting and backing up that information is your responsibility.
  • No Third-Party Sharing: We do not and cannot sell, share, transfer, or disclose your data to third parties because we do not have access to it.

4. Children's Privacy

TradingTablePro does not knowingly collect information from children. No personal information is collected, transmitted, or stored externally.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect product improvements, legal requirements, or policy updates. Any changes will be posted on this page along with an updated revision date.
