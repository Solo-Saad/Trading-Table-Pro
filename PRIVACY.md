# Privacy Policy for TradingTablePro

**Last Updated:** May 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how data is handled when you use the **TradingTablePro** application (the "Software").

We believe in complete privacy and user transparency. Because TradingTablePro is a standalone desktop application running entirely on your machine, we do not collect, monitor, store, or transmit your personal data or trading records to any external servers.

### 1. Information Collection and Storage
TradingTablePro operates purely as a local workspace tracking tool.

* **Local Storage Only:** Any information you input into the application (such as currency pairs, patterns, wave analysis, market direction, entry signals, and trade outcomes) is stored strictly on your local device.
* **Database File Path:** Your trading records are saved inside an embedded SQLite database located within your system's protected local application data directory (specifically under the `%LOCALAPPDATA%/TradingTablePro/` folder on Windows configurations).
* **No Remote Telemetry:** We do not host external databases, cloud infrastructures, or remote tracking algorithms. Your data never leaves your computer.

### 2. Information Usage
Since your trading logs remain entirely in your control on your machine, data is only handled locally by the Software to:
* Load your saved trading logs upon opening the application interface.
* Save your manually entered records cleanly when you exit the app or trigger a manual save command.
* Compute basic on-screen analytical summaries (such as your Win:Loss ratios and Bullish:Bearish transaction balances).

### 3. Data Protection and Retention
* **Your Responsibility:** Because your data is stored locally, the security of your trading history depends entirely on your system security. Deleting the application directory, wiping local application states, or formatting your storage drives will permanently clear your local records.
* **No Third-Party Sharing:** We do not, and physically cannot, sell, trade, or share your input data with third parties, advertising networks, or data brokers because we have zero access to it.

### 4. Children’s Privacy
Our Software does not knowingly contact or collect information from children. Since no personal identity tracking or remote network transmissions occur, the Software is inherently compliant with standard regional privacy laws protecting minors.

### 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may occasionally update this Privacy Policy to reflect future local software improvements or legal standard shifts. Any updates will be reflected here with a revised "Last Updated" date.
